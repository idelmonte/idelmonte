## 📂 Featured Projects

### 🏥 [Medicaid Expansion Impact Analysis]

I led a three-person analysis of complex patient-level healthcare data to evaluate the projected impact of Kansas' proposed H.A.W.K. Medicaid expansion on emergency room utilization rates, winning Best in Show among 20 teams in a 25-hour competition. We merged 5 fragmented datasets and designed a FCM clustering patient segmentation model and policy simulation pipeline, identifying a coverage-gap cohort of approximately 51,000 patients (~9.1% of sample) and projecting a reduction of 267–2,050 annual ER visits

* Tools: Python, R, scikit-learn
* Techniques: Fuzzy C-Means (FCM) Clustering (for patient segmentation), Data Merging & Wrangling (integrating 5 fragmented healthcare datasets), Project Management

### 🔥 [Survival Analysis for Predicting Wildfire Evacuation Risk](https://github.com/idelmonte/WiDS_2026)  

Operated in a remote three-person team to develop a right-censored survival modeling framework to predict the probability of a wildfire reaching an evacuation zone at 12h, 24h, 48h, and 72h horizons, handling heavily censored data with 221 training examples. Trained calibrated LightGBM models, using Brier score to optimize probability predictions and C-index to rank urgency, achieving a 0.96 AUC-ROC metric

* Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Colab
* Techniques: LightGBM (Gradient Boosting Trees), Survival Analysis frameworks (handling right-censored data), Brier Score (probability calibration), Concordance Index (C-index for urgency ranking), Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC-ROC).

### 📊 [Breast Tumor Classifier](https://github.com/idelmonte/Breast-Tumor-Classifier)
